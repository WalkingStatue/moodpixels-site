Private by design

Make space for how your day felt.

MoodPixels is a local-first mood journal. Log your day, spot patterns over time, and keep control of your own data.

Built around your data

Your journal stays on your device. There are no accounts and no cloud journal service. When you make a backup, you choose where it goes; encrypted backups use a passphrase that is never stored by MoodPixels.

A gentle daily practice

Choose one or more moods, add a note when it helps, and use your history to reflect. Optional reminders are scheduled on your device.
