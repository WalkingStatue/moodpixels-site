Privacy Policy

MoodPixels is designed to keep your mood journal on your device. This policy explains the limited information involved when you use the app and this website.

Who is responsible

MoodPixels is operated by Dhruv Saija, Gujarat, India. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@dhruvsaija.in.

Journal data

Your mood entries, notes, mood definitions, reminder preferences, and app-lock preference are stored locally on your device. MoodPixels does not provide an account system, cloud journal sync, advertising, or analytics service.

Backups you create

You may export your journal as a JSON backup. You choose the destination through your device’s share sheet. If you choose encryption, the backup is protected with a passphrase that is not sent to MoodPixels and is not recoverable if forgotten. Your chosen backup provider or destination has its own privacy practices.

Updates and tester distribution

Installed builds may check for and download app updates through Expo’s update service. Preview testers may receive an APK through Firebase App Distribution. Those services may process technical information needed to deliver the update, such as network and device information. MoodPixels does not send your journal entries or notes to them.

Permissions

Notifications are used only for an optional daily reminder. Media-library access is requested only when you choose to save a generated mood-grid image. Biometric authentication, when enabled, is handled by your device to protect access to the app; MoodPixels does not receive your biometric data.

Children

MoodPixels is intended for people aged 18 and over. Do not use the app if you are under 18.

Your choices

You can delete local journal data from the app, turn reminders and app lock off, and create or delete backups you control. Because MoodPixels has no account or cloud journal database, there is no server-side journal profile to delete.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The current version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
